Satellite Fire-Risk Assessment & Pilot Siting
QABS
Wildfire Risk Atlas
A corridor-scale fire-risk map and a 30-node pilot siting plan for the Kroumirie‑Mogods forests, built entirely on public satellite imagery and open geodata: Sentinel-2 burn history 2020–2026, ESA WorldCover fuels, terrain, OpenStreetMap ignition points and ERA5 fire weather.
Prepared for: QABS – Ignition-Stage Wildfire Detection and Last-Mile Community Alerting
Study area: Kroumirie–Mogods + Sejnane, North-West Tunisia
Pilot zone: Nefza – Cap Negro · 100 m grid · UTM 32N
